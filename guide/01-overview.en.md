## Problem and Background

Design a basic blog service where users can sign up, then write and manage their own posts.

## Target Users and Key Stakeholders

- Member: A user who has signed up and writes and manages their own posts
- Visitor: A user who reads public posts

## Core Value

Members can write and manage their posts in one place, while visitors can read public posts.

## Product Goals and Success Criteria

The product goal of this example project is achieved when all of the following capabilities are available.

1. A user can sign up.
2. A member can create their own posts.
3. Members and visitors can view public posts.
4. A member can modify their own posts.
5. A member can delete their own posts.

Members cannot modify or delete posts written by other members.

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Member | A user who has completed sign-up |
| Visitor | A user who reads public posts, regardless of whether they have signed up |
| Post | An article written by a member and published on the blog |
| Post CRUD | Creating, reading, updating, and deleting posts |
